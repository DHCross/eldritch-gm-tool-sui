Build Profile
  • Spirit Points lean lower: mental/arcane pressure may be challenging, freeing CP for other areas.
  • Active DP leans lower: dodging/parrying may be difficult. This build likely relies on passive defense or positioning.
  • Passive DP leans lower: sustained physical punishment may overwhelm. Relies more on avoiding hits entirely.
  • Perception is lightly invested: relies on caution or allies to spot traps/ambushes.
  • Ranged capability is lightly invested: prefers close-quarters or magic engagements.
